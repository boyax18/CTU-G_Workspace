## 1. Reviewed target and boundaries

- Actor: public Student applicant; no account or application permission required.
- Trigger: applicant submits the Student registration form to request email verification.
- Target preconditions: public registration available, valid CSRF protection, Student requests available, protected staging and OTP delivery available.
- Target result: after a valid OTP, exactly one Pending/inactive account and linked profile, with durable verification proof and no assigned role, grants or authenticated session.
- School Admin approval and academic eligibility review belong to WF-02. OTP proves access to an email address; it does not prove enrollment.
- Existing academic fields are configured later; Course, Year Level and Section are not registration inputs.
- WF-01.02 and other workflow specifications were not advanced by this review.

The catalogue checklist now ticks Inspected, Reconciled and Cleaned only. Verified is unchecked because rendered-page inspection and target OTP acceptance have not been completed. Individual verified source/DB findings below do not certify a working OTP flow or human approval.
