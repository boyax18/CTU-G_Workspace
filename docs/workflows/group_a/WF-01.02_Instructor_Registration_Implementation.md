## Cleaned behavior

Uncertain Instructor writes previously offered Check registration status, which called the Student-only cookie-bound OTP endpoint and could not determine whether a direct Instructor registration committed. The page now:

- Provides explicit guidance: We could not confirm whether your Instructor registration completed. Check with your School Admin before submitting again.
- Clears passwords, keeps safe fields in memory and prevents edits/submission until an explicit restart. The role cannot be changed to reinterpret an unresolved Instructor request as a Student attempt.
- Offers no Student OTP status action for Instructor uncertainty; the recovery handler also guards against calling it for Instructor.
- Performs no automatic retry or identity-status lookup. Restart itself makes no request and preserves Instructor selection and safe input.
- Preserves Student's explicit cookie-bound status recovery, verified by a browser regression test.

Registration success focus was changed from a zero-delay timer to an effect tied to submitted state. Backend application logic and schema were not changed. Additional backend test assertions establish that direct Instructor registration neither creates nor claims OTP proof.
